A verdict records existing evidence. **VERIFIED** means an existing proof audit covers the statement, including any specified dependencies or repairs. **NOT-BROKEN** means an isolated examination found no refutation. **UNAUDITED** means that an independent proof verdict for the stated claim is missing. Where a source reports a computational check, a credit review or an internal conclusion, the row names it. Placement records the requested paper structure and the limits of the available proofs. The owner holds the release decision.

A *rigid* matrix has signed **side-preserving** automorphism group exactly $\{(I,I),(-I,-I)\}$. Signed equivalence allows independent signed row and column permutations. The class counts below also identify transposes. A *good* output has intrinsically recovered point fibres on both axes. In a fixed ensemble $L$ counts labellings, $D$ distinct output arrays, $C$ classes meeting the image and $C_{\rm rigid}$ classes containing a good rigid output. Logarithms in the estimates are natural. $H(n)$ denotes existence of a real Hadamard matrix of order $n$.

## What the two instances share

The basis change uses orthogonal local frames (a local Hadamard matrix divided by the square root of its order). If the row and column point fibres have sizes $h_x,h_y$, a tile of the physical sign matrix has the form $\epsilon_{xy} f_p g_q^T$ for two unnormalized sign columns. Its tile in $W=\widehat F^T H\widehat G$ has one entry of magnitude $\sqrt{h_xh_y}$. The bipartite flag graph has vertices for the ports at the point occurrences on each side and one edge for each such tile. In the square instance the *support* is a perfect matching. With orthogonally normalized frames its nonzeros have magnitude $m$.

## Meisner's square

At $(i,j)$ the tile uses column $p_i(j)$ of $A$ and row $p'_j(i)$ of $A$, with their assigned signs. In the **Bush ensemble** $A$ is normalized, both diagonal ports use index 1, and both diagonal port signs are $+1$. The comparison ensemble in B01 has a different diagonal convention.

Each ordered pair of distinct-group points lies on one line and, in each group $i$, $L\mapsto(\ell_i(L),\ell_i(\pi L))$ is bijective onto ordered pairs of group points. Use the displayed skew conference $K$ of order four. Unless indicated otherwise, fix one Hadamard $A$ of order $h$, use $A^T$ on row points and $A$ on column points, and choose all signed port permutations independently and uniformly at the $8h$ point occurrences. Write $N=4h^2$, $L=(2^h h!)^{8h}$ for the number of labellings, and $a=(2h)^{\lceil2\log_2h\rceil+2}$.
