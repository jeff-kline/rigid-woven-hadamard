# Proof audit of the four-group line involution

STAGE: PROOF-AUDIT  
VERDICT: VERIFIED for the first section's mathematical statements. One citation number in packet v1 needs correction.

CLAIM: Real Hadamard inputs on four groups of a transversal design equipped with the stated common line involution yield a signable regular Hadamard of order 4h². The explicit field-product supply expands both earlier order images, has distinct-order exponent 1/2, and only produces orders with independent regular witnesses.

**Flag calculation.** The listed K has transpose −K and Gram 3I. The flag operators C and P commute because they act on different coordinates. P is symmetric and orthogonal. Thus (C+P)(C+P)ᵀ=4I. The left and right changes of basis are orthogonal, even if their local bases or incident-line orderings differ. A point pair in different groups has exactly one contributing flag pair from C. An ordered point pair in the same group, including a pair with identical entries, has exactly one such contribution from P. Each resulting block is a signed outer product of unnormalized Hadamard columns. Its entries are signs and the scaled Gram is 4h²I.

**Regularity.** If π sends a line through x to another line through x, both lines yield the ordered pair (x,x), hence are equal. The h fixed lines therefore partition the points, since there is exactly one per point. The right signing picks the G-column for that line at each point, multiplied by its group sign dᵢ. Its normalized transform vanishes off the fixed flags. Direct multiplication verifies (K+I)(−1,1,1,1)ᵀ=(2,2,2,2)ᵀ on every fixed line. The resulting unsigned row-sum magnitudes are 2h, and the prescribed left row signing makes every sum +2h. Orthogonality gives the same column sums.

**Supply and counting.** On the projective line over F_q the dual swap fixes just the slope 1 in even characteristic and the slopes ±1 in odd characteristic. The remaining q or q−1 directions include four whenever the packet requires them. A nonfixed direction and its swapped image give an invertible two-dimensional linear system. Two distinct directions also give an invertible system. Both bijections persist coordinatewise in the product. The four listed directions work in F₄ and F₅. The 5-adic valuation of 20·2^m is always one, so these h are nonsquares. Paley I gives H_20 and all H_(p+1) in the progression, and tensor doubling gives the other specified local orders. In the progression p≡7 mod 12, h is divisible by four and prime to three. With u=√X/2−1, the injective subfamily has π(u;12,7) ~ u/(4 log u) ~ √X/(4 log X). All regular Hadamard orders are integer squares by orthogonality and constant row sum. These two bounds give the claimed exponent, while leaving relative density among squares open in this argument. [Bennett, Martin, O'Bryant and Rechnitzer, Corollary 1.6](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/BeMaObRe-IJM-2018.pdf)

**Previous order images.** The earlier paired source gives (k−1)t⁴+2t² for even t and k, of valuation 2ν₂(t)+1. The earlier dyadic source gives N=4^(j+a)[1+(k−1)4^(j−a)] for k a power of two and a≤j. If a=j this is a power of two. Otherwise its odd part minus one has valuation 2(j−a), as opposed to ν₂(25−1)=3. Hence all 1600·4^m are outside both prior images. The old paired exponent follows from the reviewed divisor bound on k and its fixed-k lower bound. Three dyadic parameters each contribute O(log X) possibilities in the wider branch. In particular deleting all older orders from the prime progression removes at most O(log³ X) values, preserving its asymptotic.

**Independent prior order witness.** The double-index identity (A⊗A)vec(A)=vec(A A Aᵀ)=h vec(A) follows by expanding the two indices. Sign both axes by vec(A) and tensor with J₄−2I₄. This is regular of order 4h². The proof makes no comparison of equivalence classes and gives no historical priority. Kharaghani's independent Bush construction appears in **Theorem 1**, printed p. 3 of the cited Bonato–Holzmann–Kharaghani paper. [Published article](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v8i1r1/pdf)
